Kanwar Jit Singh Gambhir

Founder & Creative Director

Kanwar has spent over 25 years making print look right. He learned ink, pen and brush before computers entered the studio, and inherited his love of art from his father, a gold-medalist artist. Today he is an Art Director who knows every step from first sketch to press-ready file, with school textbook series, magazines, ad campaigns and a United Nations Environment Programme report behind him. He has also built workflows and trained design teams at Techbooks (now Aptara), Thomson Digital and Express KCS. With Illustrator, InDesign, Photoshop, CorelDraw and QuarkXPress at his fingertips, he keeps every Graphico job sharp, clean and print-perfect.

Isac Anugreh Gambhir
