Privacy notice

When you send a project enquiry through this website, Graphico receives the details you provide, such as your name, contact information, organisation, project requirements and any optional artwork or reference file.

How we use your details

We use your enquiry to understand the project, prepare or discuss a quote, and contact you about the work. We do not ask you to provide sensitive personal information; please leave it out of the form and any uploaded file.

Form service and notifications

The website form is processed by Netlify Forms. Submitted details and optional files are available to Graphico through the Netlify dashboard, and email notifications may be sent to help us respond. Netlify currently limits each form request, including an upload, to 8 MB.

How long we keep enquiries

We keep enquiry records while we are working on the quote or project. We delete them when they are no longer needed for follow-up or providing the service, unless we need to retain them for a legal or business record obligation.

External page resources

This page uses fonts from Google Fonts and two studio photographs served by Pexels. When your browser loads those resources, it connects to those services.

Questions or deletion requests

To ask a question about an enquiry or request that it be deleted, use the project enquiry form and mention “privacy request” in your message. We will use the contact details you provide to respond.

This notice applies to project enquiries submitted through this website. It may be updated as Graphico’s website services and contact channels change.
