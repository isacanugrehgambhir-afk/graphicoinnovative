1. Scope of Services & Additional Charges

  • Each package covers only the services and deliverables explicitly mentioned under the respective bundle.
  • Any additional requirements, design variations, deliverables or modifications beyond the agreed package scope will be quoted separately, based on the nature and complexity of the work.
  • Additional stationery items, creative adaptations, design formats, pages, slides or packaging variations not included in the selected package will attract additional charges.
  • Any change in the original project brief or creative direction after work has commenced may be treated as additional work and charged accordingly.
  • All additional work will be undertaken only after the client's approval of the revised quotation.

2. Revision Policy

Each package includes a predefined number of revision rounds to ensure that the final deliverables align with the agreed creative brief.

  • Visualizer: 1 round of consolidated revisions.
  • Appetizer: 3 rounds of consolidated revisions.
  • Business Elevate: 5 rounds of consolidated revisions.
  • A revision round refers to one consolidated set of feedback covering the submitted designs. Minor adjustments to typography, colours, alignment, layout and other existing design elements are considered revisions.
  • Any changes involving a new creative direction, substantial redesign, change in the original brief or additional deliverables will be considered outside the included revision allowance and charged separately.
  • Additional revision rounds, wherever required, will be quoted separately.

3. Exclusions & Client Responsibilities

  • Printing, physical production, packaging manufacturing, shipping and delivery charges are not included in the package prices and will be quoted separately.
  • The client is responsible for providing all necessary content, business information, product details, photographs, text and other reference materials required for the project, unless otherwise agreed.
  • Stock images, premium fonts, licensed illustrations, paid assets and other third-party resources, wherever required, will be charged separately with prior client approval.
  • Content writing, copywriting, photography, videography, brand strategy and other services not explicitly listed under the selected package are excluded.
  • Editable source files and working files will be provided only where specifically agreed upon in the project quotation.
  • Each package is applicable to one business or brand. Additional brands, business entities or product lines will be quoted separately.

4. Payment Terms

  • A 50% advance payment is required to confirm the project and initiate work.
  • The remaining 50% balance is payable upon completion and approval of the agreed deliverables, prior to the release of final high-resolution print files and applicable source files.
  • Any additional work or services requested beyond the original package scope will be quoted separately and must be approved before execution.
  • Applicable taxes, including GST wherever applicable, will be charged additionally.
  • Project commencement is subject to receipt of the advance payment and the necessary client inputs.

5. Project Timelines & Delivery

  • Estimated project timelines will be communicated upon confirmation of the selected package and will depend on the scope and complexity of the deliverables.
  • Timelines commence once the advance payment and all necessary content, information and reference materials have been received.
  • Delays in providing feedback, approvals or required inputs from the client's end may result in corresponding extensions to the original delivery schedule.
  • Final deliverables will be shared digitally in the agreed formats once the outstanding payment has been cleared.
  • Physical printing, production and dispatch, if required, will follow a separately agreed timeline.
  • Any significant changes to the project scope during execution may require a revised delivery schedule.

6. Design Approval & Ownership

  • The client is responsible for reviewing and approving all designs, content, spelling, product information, pricing and other details before final approval.
  • Once the final designs have been approved and delivered, any subsequent modifications will be treated as additional work.
  • Ownership and agreed usage rights for the final approved designs will be transferred to the client upon receipt of full payment.
  • Third-party assets, fonts, stock imagery and licensed elements remain subject to their respective licensing terms.
